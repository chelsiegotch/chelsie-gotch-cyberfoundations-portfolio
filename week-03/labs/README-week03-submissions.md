All five of these are graded — submitted through the Lab Portal and checked against your repo. None of them is a flagship GitHub Deliverable, though — that's a separate portfolio checkpoint that starts in Week 4.

How you'll submit: Fill out each lab's, notes', and reflection's worksheet in the Lab Portal, then click Submit to GitHub — it commits the completed file to your repo automatically for all five of these. You do not need to manually create notes.md or reflection.md yourself; the Portal creates them for you the first time you submit. Screenshots of your CLI Simulator sessions are optional for all three labs.

Three separate things: (1) finishing simulator challenges saves your challenge progress in the simulator; (2) Save and Submit to GitHub on each worksheet send your written answers to your repo; (3) the lab tracker is a separate site where you log that you're done. Doing one does not do the others. After submitting each item, also log it as complete in the lab tracker at labsubmission.cybervisionariesinstitute.org — separate from the Lab Portal, and how your completion gets recorded.
